Unusual Noises from the Plumbing: Gurgling or bubbling sounds coming from your toilets or sinks suggest that air is trapped within your sewer lines due to a blockage or break. In such cases, a sewer camera inspection provides a detailed view of the issue, allowing for accurate diagnosis and repair. Foul Odors Emerging from Drains: Bad smells emanating from your drains are often indicative of sewer line issues, such as cracks or blockages that trap debris and emit odors. An inspection with a sewer camera will reveal these issues, enabling swift addressing and odor elimination. Recent Purchases of Older Homes: If you have recently acquired an older property, scheduling a sewer camera inspection is a wise decision.
