Tips to Prevent Drain Clogs at Home Protect your plumbing system by practicing these simple habits: Use Drain Screens: Prevent debris like hair, food scraps, and soap chunks from entering your drains. Avoid Pouring Grease Down the Sink: Grease solidifies and forms tough blockages—dispose of it in the trash instead. Flush Drains Regularly: Run hot water to break down build-up and maintain flow. Limit Chemicals: Harsh cleaners wear out pipes—use enzyme-based drain treatments for safe cleaning.
